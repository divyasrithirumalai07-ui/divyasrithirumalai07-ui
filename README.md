**PhD Research Scholar | Clinical Nutrition & Biomedical Sciences**

* **Microbiome & Metabolomics:** Gut microbial ecology, microbial profiling, and metabolite analysis
* **Computational Biology:** *In-silico* screening, ligand-receptor molecular docking, and pathway exploration
* **Molecular & Preclinical Science:** Real-time gene expression analysis (RT-qPCR), inflammatory pathways, and oxidative stress biomarkers
